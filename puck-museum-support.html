Quick help

  • How to play: Drag to aim, release to fire. Clear the exhibits before the hall advances.
  • Floor 2: Some exhibits fade — hit them while they are solid.
  • Floor 3+: Brass edges block the puck — aim for the open side.
  • Free galleries: Shang and Egypt are free to play.
  • Other galleries: Optional one-time unlocks, or the Full Museum pack.

Purchases & restore

If you reinstall the App or get a new device, use Restore Purchases in the App (store / settings area) while signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Payment issues (charges, refunds, family sharing) are handled by Apple. You can also report a problem via reportaproblem.apple.com.
