Frequently asked questions

What's included for free?
Every JLPT level (N5 through N1) comes with a free preview — a handful of real vocabulary and grammar cards you can study, quiz yourself on, and get a feel for the app with. No account, no time limit, no ads.

What does the full unlock include?
A one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks the complete vocabulary and grammar library across all five levels, plus the ability to bulk-import your own card lists from a CSV file. Pay once, it's yours for good.

I purchased the unlock on another device — how do I get it back?
Open Manage → Settings and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID you originally purchased with, this restores it immediately at no extra charge.

Can I use the app in a language other than English?
Yes — go to Manage → Settings → App Language and pick from English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, or Vietnamese. This changes the app's own interface; it's independent of whatever language your phone's overall system is set to.

Why is the dictionary still in English even though I changed the app's language?
The app's menus, buttons, and screens are translated, but the actual Japanese vocabulary definitions come from a Japanese-English dictionary (JMdict) — there isn't a reliable, equally accurate Japanese-to-[your language] dictionary of the same scale to draw from, so word meanings currently stay in English regardless of your chosen app language. This is a deliberate choice to avoid showing lower-quality or machine-translated definitions.

How does the daily reminder work?
Turn it on in Manage → Settings, pick a time, and you'll get a gentle daily nudge to review. If it doesn't seem to be firing, check iOS Settings → JL Chat PT → Notifications and make sure notifications are allowed — the in-app toggle can only ask for permission once, so if it was denied early on, you'll need to re-enable it there directly.

How does spaced repetition (SRS) work here?
After you study a card, you grade how well you knew it — Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. That grade decides how many days until the app shows you that card again: sooner if you struggled, later if it was easy. Reviewing right before you'd naturally forget something is what makes it stick.

Can I add my own words or grammar points?
Yes — use Add a card on any level screen to add one by hand, or Manage → Import to bulk-add many at once from a CSV file (a one-time purchase feature).

Is my data backed up anywhere?
Your progress lives entirely on your own device — there's no account or cloud sync. Use Manage → Export to save a backup file of your custom cards and progress, which you can keep somewhere safe or use to move your data to a new device.

Does this app work offline?
Yes, completely. Studying, quizzing, and dictionary lookup all work with no internet connection — everything is bundled with the app.
