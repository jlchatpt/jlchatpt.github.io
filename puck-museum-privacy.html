Puck: The Museum — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Puck: The Museum (“the App”) is an iOS arcade game. This page explains what data the App handles and confirms that we do not sell or monetize personal data through ads or trackers.

The short version

The App stores game progress on your device. Purchases go through Apple. There is no account to create, no ad network, and no analytics SDK that profiles you for advertising.

What the App stores on your device

Depending on how you play, the App may keep locally:

  • High scores and furthest floor reached
  • Which galleries you have unlocked
  • Simple preferences (for example, sound on/off)
  • Flags used only to show one-time gameplay tips

This information stays on your device (for example in app preferences). It is not uploaded to a developer-operated game server, because the App does not use one for saves.

In-app purchases

Optional gallery unlocks and the Full Museum unlock are sold as non-consumable in-app purchases. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card number. Apple may provide us limited purchase records (for example, that a product was bought) so the App can unlock content and support Restore Purchases.

Apple’s handling of payment data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad-tracking SDKs
  • No third-party analytics SDKs used to build advertising profiles
  • No account system and no password stored by us
  • We do not sell your personal information to data brokers

Internet use

The App needs a network connection for App Store / StoreKit features (loading products, purchasing, and restoring purchases). Gameplay itself does not require you to send personal content to a developer server.

Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own databases. Purchases on a device follow the device’s App Store and Family Sharing / Ask to Buy settings controlled by Apple and the device owner.

Your control

  • Delete local data: removing the App from your device deletes the App’s local storage on that device.
  • Purchases: managed in your Apple ID / App Store settings; use Restore Purchases in the App if you reinstall.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
