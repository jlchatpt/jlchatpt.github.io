Privacy Policy

Bronze Sky · last updated 8 August 2026

Bronze Sky does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The game does not require an internet connection to play.

Data stored on your device

Your high scores, settings and whether you have unlocked the endless waves are saved on your device only. This information is never sent anywhere, and it is removed if you delete the app.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, the score you achieve is submitted to Apple's Game Center service so it can appear on the leaderboard, along with the player identifier and nickname that Apple assigns to you. This is handled entirely by Apple; I never receive your real name, email address or Apple Account details, and I cannot see anything beyond the public leaderboard that every other player can see.

Game Center is optional. If you decline it, the game keeps a leaderboard stored only on your device and nothing is transmitted. Apple's handling of Game Center data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or handle your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid, which it stores on your device so the content stays unlocked.

Children

Bronze Sky is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal data is collected from any user, no special provisions are required.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
